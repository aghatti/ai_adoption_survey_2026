# Methodology
## Objective

This study aims to understand how people use AI tools in practice, what limits broader adoption, how spending behavior differs across user groups, and what users expect from AI over the next few years.

## Scope

The survey focuses on three contexts:
- Personal use
- Employee productivity
- Business implementation
 
## Design
- Cross-sectional online survey
- Anonymous participation
- Self-reported responses
- Convenience sampling

## Research Focus

The study explores:
- AI adoption patterns
- Tool usage
- Spending behavior
- Barriers and frustrations
- Business implementation constraints
- Future expectations

## Transparency

The researcher behind this study is involved in building AI products.

To reduce bias:
- No product-specific validation questions are included.
- No feature prioritization questions are included.
- No pricing optimization framework is used.

Results will be published independently of any product roadmap.

## Limitations
- Self-reported behavior may differ from actual behavior.
- The sample is not population-representative.
- Results should be interpreted as descriptive observations rather than population-level estimates.
