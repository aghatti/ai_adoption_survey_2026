# Cross-Segment AI Adoption &amp; Constraint Mapping Study (2026)
## About
Everyone talks about AI.

Far fewer discussions are backed by data about how people actually use AI in their daily lives and work.

This study aims to map:
- Which AI tools people use
- How frequently they use them
- What they pay for AI
- What limits wider adoption
- How businesses are implementing AI
- What users expect from AI over the next 3–10 years

The project focuses on three groups:
1. Individual users
2. Employees using AI for productivity
3. Business owners and leaders implementing AI in business processes

The goal is not to promote a product or validate a business idea.

The goal is to create an open snapshot of AI adoption patterns, constraints, and expectations in 2026.

## Research Questions
- How do AI adoption patterns differ between consumers, employees, and business implementers?
- What are the biggest barriers to broader AI usage?
- How do users think about pricing, subscriptions, and value?
- How common are issues such as model deprecation, changing limits, and subscription fatigue?
- What do people expect AI to become over the next few years?

## Methodology
- Cross-sectional online survey
- Anonymous participation
- Convenience sampling
- English and Russian versions
- Public reporting of aggregated results

The study collects descriptive data only.

It does not attempt to estimate market size, forecast future markets, or make population-level claims.

## Transparency

The researcher behind this study is also building products in the AI space.

To reduce bias:
- No product-specific questions are included.
- No feature validation questions are included.
- No pricing optimization framework is used.
- Results will be published independently from any product roadmap.
